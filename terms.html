2. The game

StreetKeep is a collecting game: you obtain places in France (addresses, named places, monuments), you build on them, you trade them with other players, and you can become Mayor of your town in the game.

Everything happens in the game. Owning a place in StreetKeep gives no right over the real place, nor over any real property. The places in the game are virtual items, over which you only receive a personal right of use within the game. The game has no connection with the owners, occupants or managers of the real places shown.

The game is free. Some items can be obtained with gems, bought with real money (part 6).

Automated players may take part in the game.

Treasure hunts are free and open to all players who sign up for the hunt. Everyone starts from the same place and moves at the same speed; no purchase helps to win. The reward (coins and a randomly drawn place) stays in the game and has no monetary value. The publisher may cancel a win obtained by cheating.

Coins and gems have no monetary value. They are not refundable, not convertible into money, not exchangeable for coins (for gems), and not transferable outside the game.

To display it, Google sets and reads its own cookies on your device (to run and secure its service, measure its audience and, depending on your Google settings, personalise ads). The game can’t display the street view without them, and the publisher can’t choose them on Google’s behalf.

By playing StreetKeep, you accept Google’s cookies related to the street view. If you don’t accept them, don’t play the game (you can delete your account at any time).

You can delete Google’s cookies in your browser settings, and adjust Google’s ad personalisation at myadcenter.google.com. See also Google’s page on its cookies.
