Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This is an English translation for convenience. The French version is the reference text.

In short

We collect the minimum needed to run the game: your e-mail, your username and your game. No advertising, no resale of data, no advertising tracker. You can delete your account at any time from the game (your profile → “Delete my account”).

Who is responsible for your data?

Gautier Lagrée, sole trader (entrepreneur individuel, SIREN 941 648 156), 25 rue Jean Marin Naudin, 92220 Bagneux, France. Contact: contact@citydeck.fr.

What data, and why?

  • Your account: e-mail address and password (the password is never stored in plain text). To log you in and secure your account.
  • Your public profile: username, icon, sign-up date. Visible to other players.
  • Your game: your places, your buildings, your coins and gems, your offers, sales, trades, auction bids, payments into town funds, your ranking, the date of your last visit (some game rules depend on it) and the days you came to play (to measure how much the game is used, never shown to other players). Owned places and rankings are visible to other players.
  • Your purchases: the offer bought, its price, its date, the country of the bank card (packs are not sold in some countries) and the consent you give to receive your gems immediately. We never see your card numbers: payment is made by Stripe.
  • The reports you send about other players, for moderation.
  • Technical information: as for any website, the servers hosting the game record the IP address and the date of connections, for security.
  • During a treasure hunt: the position of your piece on the game map (not your real location), every 2 seconds, to check the race and name the winner. Other nearby hunters see your piece, your nickname and your icon.
  • Against cheating: a fingerprint of your connection, once a day. It is an encrypted digest of your IP address, from which the address can’t be recovered. It is used to spot accounts created in series. The server also counts your actions per minute, to stop programs that play in a player’s place.

Legal bases: performance of the contract (providing you with the game and your purchases), compliance with legal obligations (sales accounting), and the publisher’s legitimate interest (security, fighting cheating, moderation).

What is stored on your device

The game stores in your browser (local storage) your login session, your settings (sounds, vibrations, language…) and a copy of your game, in order to work. These are not advertising trackers: they don’t require your consent.

The street view is provided by Google Street View, which sets and reads its own cookies to display it. It is part of the game and can’t be displayed without them: by playing, you accept these Google cookies (see the terms, part 10, and Google’s policy). The game itself sets no advertising cookie.

Who receives your data?

No one other than the publisher and its technical providers, who act only to run the game:

  • Supabase (accounts and games; data hosted in the European Union, in Paris);
  • Stripe (payment for gems);
  • GitHub (hosting of the site’s pages);
  • Brevo (sending login e-mails: sign-up confirmation, forgotten password; a French company, data in the European Union);
  • the map and image services the page loads directly from your device: IGN (maps), Google (street view) and the game’s libraries (unpkg).

Some of these providers are located outside the European Union (United States). Transfers are covered by the safeguards provided by the GDPR (adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses of the European Commission).

Your data is never sold or used for advertising.

For how long?

  • Your account and your game: as long as your account exists. They are erased when you delete your account.
  • Payment records: for the time required by law (10 years for accounting), at Stripe and at the publisher.
  • Technical logs: a few weeks at most, at the host.
  • Connection fingerprints (against cheating): 30 days.
  • The positions of your piece during a treasure hunt: 7 days after the hunt ends.

Your rights

You can at any time access your data, correct it, have it erased, ask to receive a copy, object to a processing or ask for it to be restricted. The simplest way to erase everything: your profile → “Delete my account”. For anything else, write to contact@citydeck.fr; we reply within one month.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can contact the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).

Minors

The game is open to all. Under 15, registration must be done with a parent’s consent. A minor may only buy gems with their parents’ consent.
