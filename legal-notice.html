Data and content used

The game shows real places in France based on public and open data, for game purposes only:

  • buildings, roads and plots: French National Institute of Geographic and Forest Information (IGN), BD TOPO® and Plan cadastral informatisé, under the Etalab 2.0 open licence;
  • addresses: Base Adresse Nationale (BAN), under the Etalab 2.0 open licence;
  • property sale prices: Demandes de valeurs foncières (DVF), under the Etalab 2.0 open licence;
  • famous places and monuments: Wikidata (public domain, CC0);
  • street views: Google Street View, embedded under Google’s terms.

Owning a place in the game gives no right over the real place. The game has no connection with the owners, occupants or managers of the places shown.
